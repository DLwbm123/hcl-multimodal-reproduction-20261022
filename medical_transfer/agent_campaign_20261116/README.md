## What the evidence supports

The adaptive study actually ran two original HCL feedback batches for each matched method. Both accepted memory candidates; the new method also accepted one prompt update. Experience can help relative to empty memory on that panel, but the tested applicability features and controller statistics did not improve the prespecified matched comparison. This is more limited than claiming a new continual-learning algorithm works.

The routing candidate retained independent observations plus training-derived output contracts only for training-supported color/location routes. Its three-point confirmation gain is suggestive; the small discordance count and interval including zero do not establish reliable superiority. Contract-only arbitration subsequently had no supported training override. That training rejection does not prove that independent observations caused the previous confirmation gain.

The region skill is real execution, not a simulated crop: legal proposals map to original pixels, actual crops are read, and absent/invalid proposals fall back within the same two-tool budget. Nevertheless, its training gain occurred in fallback; matched crop/fullview training controls found no accuracy difference; independent confirmation did not improve. Do not retain this module as a proven contribution solely because it is implemented.

Random storage identifiers were an unintended model input. The deterministic removal of known artifact-name fields preserves clinical evidence and private receipts; runtime guards confirmed zero exposure in cleaned runs. Previous semantic tool outputs differing only by filenames still do not establish filename causality for a particular answer change. The repair is engineering hygiene, not scientific novelty or a retroactive correction of old results.

## Data boundary and current decision

The final reserved100 passed from action to arbitration, then to grounding as a fixed selection24/confirmation76 partition. Grounding evaluated both partitions. Their historical source directories do not make them unused again. They cannot be reshuffled or used for prompt, threshold, seed, routing or model selection. A metadata-only feasibility check previously could not allocate another balanced independent24/48 panel with the required source-group isolation. Official test remains unused; patient independence is unverified.

The necessary crop and input-consistency diagnostics are complete. There is no justified continuation of infrastructure-only experiments as a substitute for the method objective. No further model runs are launched from the current exhausted validation pool. A new efficacy experiment requires a genuinely independent, source-isolated evaluation allocation and a protocol fixed before inspecting outcomes; any new mechanism requires training/development justification, rather than tuning to revealed confirmation. No externally sourced data or broader access has been added.

## Costs and delivery scope

Nine complete study lifetimes account for **65,846 issued generations**; stable includes three interrupted calls lacking completed receipts. Their recorded per-run storage snapshots sum to **440,524,800 bytes**. These totals include experiments begun before the twelve-hour authorization window: they are not newly incurred window-only costs. Runs overlap, so their durations must not be summed as campaign elapsed time. Generation and feature-forward costs are distinct; shared-GPU timing does not prove speedups.

All nine studies are delivered as source, sanitized protocols, aggregate results and reports. Viewcontrol preserves its original postprocessing failure and documents zero-inference recovery; other completion details remain in each report. Private medical images/crops, questions/labels, memberships, per-case traces/boxes, detector outputs, learned states and credentials are excluded. Runtime still depends on existing authorized model, data and HCL adapter packages; this is not a self-contained public data reproduction bundle.
